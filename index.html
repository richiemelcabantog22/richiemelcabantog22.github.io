About me

I turn ideas into software that actually runs.

I'm a full-stack developer who likes taking a feature from a rough idea to a working build — no hand-offs, no loose ends.

Most of my work lives at the intersection of mobile and realtime data: Flutter apps with offline storage, Node.js and Express APIs, MongoDB models, and Socket.IO channels that keep everything in sync. I've built a smart transport tracker, an AR folklore app, an on-device drowsiness detector, and a community portal with live incident mapping.

I also work with AI-assisted tooling as part of my development workflow. It helps me move faster and explore solutions — but every line still gets reviewed, tested, and debugged before it ships. Troubleshooting is the part I enjoy most.
